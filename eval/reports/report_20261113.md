### Did the two known wrong cases improve?

**Yes, one of two:**

1. **`No Shipment ccaptured_png...` (stale overlay, FIXED).** Root cause was deeper than originally diagnosed: it wasn't *only* `label_detect` misidentifying the bright platform as a label region - `overlay.py`'s `non_overlay_lines()` also had two regex gaps that let the overlay's own "0217X0143X0168" dimension value and a duplicate "Bengal)" OCR fragment leak through as false "independent text" evidence, on top of `parcel_present` wrongly trusting the bogus label region alone. Fixed all three: `parcel_present` no longer trusts a label region by itself (only an actual barcode or actual independent text), and the two regex gaps are closed. Now correctly predicts `FLAGGED/NO_PARCEL` with the specific reason "Sorter overlay reports AWB 19108610815135 but no parcel is visible in the frame."
2. **`Multiple shipment packets...` (two parcels, no decoded barcode, NOT FIXED).** Implemented two new deterministic signals this phase (`distinct_label_regions`, `distinct_ocr_awb_regions`) specifically to catch this gap. Neither fires on this image: the second parcel ("MYFITNESS" box) has no barcode, no legible AWB-shaped number, and its bright surface didn't survive as a second qualifying label-shaped region alongside the first ("Sparify" box's) region - the only independent evidence of the second parcel is its *brand name text* ("MYFITNE\"", "Explore"), which isn't something a region-counting or AWB-number-counting heuristic can use. This is a genuine limit of deterministic heuristics, not a bug - see "what the free path can and cannot distinguish" in the Phase 5 report.

### A new trade-off surfaced by this fix (reported honestly, not hidden)

Tightening `parcel_present` to not trust a label region alone fixed the stale-overlay case but cost one genuine OK: `Test in red color_png...` has a real parcel, but its *only* legible text in the whole frame is the overlay (duplicated twice), with no barcode and no independent OCR text - textually indistinguishable from the stale-overlay case. It's now `FLAGGED/NO_PARCEL` instead of correctly `OK`. This is the explicit, intended trade-off direction per this project's own priority ("a wrong AWB is worse than a flag") - the alternative (keep trusting the label region) is exactly what caused the stale-overlay false-OK in the first place. Net effect across all 38 images: one case moved from wrong-OK to correctly-flagged (good), one case moved from correctly-OK to flagged-but-actually-fine (an honest, explained cost), and the aggregate wrong-extraction rate still dropped.

Full detail, including the robustness-augmentation results, in the Phase 5 report delivered separately and `eval/reports/robustness_20261114.md`.

## Phase 6: a further refinement after testing at full-dataset scale

Phase 6's edge-case walkthrough included re-running the pipeline on the full 262-image `sample_data/raw` dataset (not just the 38 ground-truth images) - this surfaced something the 38-image eval set was too small to reveal.

**Finding:** 69/262 images (26%) hit the `not scene.parcel_present` / stale-overlay branch introduced in Phase 5. Every single one was the same pattern: a sorter-overlay-only read with zero independent corroboration (no barcode, no non-overlay OCR text) - not a visibly empty frame. Of those 69, 17 had a `label_region` found by `label_detect.py` (i.e. *something* solid-shaped is in the photo, just unconfirmed) and 52 had no label region at all.

**Change:** the 17 "overlay-only but a label region was found" cases now return `LOW_CONFIDENCE_READ` (overlay AWB as `awb_suggestion`, never `awb`) instead of a flat `NO_PARCEL` - a label region being found is weaker evidence than independent text, but it's stronger than nothing, and `LOW_CONFIDENCE_READ` is the flag that already exists for exactly this "a candidate exists but evidence is too weak to trust" situation. The other 52 (no label region either) are left as `NO_PARCEL` - there's no ground truth in this specific category to justify a further heuristic, so none was added.

**Effect on the 38-image eval set:** `NO_PARCEL` predictions dropped from 8 to 6; `LOW_CONFIDENCE_READ` predictions rose from 1 to 2. Wrong-extraction rate and silent-miss rate are **unchanged** (3.7% and 16.7% respectively) - this change only affects which *kind* of flag is shown, never whether something gets a confirmed `OK`.

**Honest cost:** this measurably regresses the flag-type (not safety) of the exact case Phase 5 targeted. `No Shipment ccaptured_png...` has a (bogus) `label_region` found, so it now lands on `LOW_CONFIDENCE_READ` instead of the correct `NO_PARCEL` - one more flag-type mismatch in the confusion matrix (`(NO_PARCEL, LOW_CONFIDENCE_READ): 1`), though still never an `OK`. This is a genuine trade-off, not a free win: the same imprecise `label_region_found` signal that caused the Phase 5 bug is exactly what distinguishes the 17 cases this change helps from the one case it makes slightly worse, and there's no way to split those two groups more precisely without better evidence than this heuristic can provide. Kept anyway because it doesn't touch the safety-critical metric (wrong-extraction rate) and gives a more honest, actionable message (a specific AWB to check, not a blanket "nothing here") for the larger group.

**Full 262-image outcome after this change:** OK 180, NO_PARCEL 52 (was 69), LOW_CONFIDENCE_READ 22 (was 5), PARTIAL_PARCEL 8, ERROR 0.
